原文情节梳理 · 四段式导图

原文情节
四段式
① 矛盾起因 — Deepak 当众挑衅：「tabla is a boy's thing」，Anjali 困惑不解
② 妥协退缩 — 听到嘘声后自我怀疑：「I guess I don't know it that well after all」
③ 冲突激化 — 想找 Deepak 说清楚却被无视；偷听到恶语中伤，Anjali 怒不可遏
④ 情绪崩溃 — 躲进洗手间无法冷静：「If I play in this concert, no one will talk to me again」

关键伏笔：鼓乐不分性别，只关乎努力与汗水（Anjali knew there was no such thing）——续写海报标语与主旨必须回扣这一点。

Writing Clinic 1

讲评① 海报内容 + Anjali 的反应

海报标语：必须扣牢文章

反思：Anjali 情绪低落的真正原因是因性别被质疑、被偏见而自我怀疑，而不是对自己没有信心。因此标语不能泛泛鼓励「加油 / 坚持」。

推荐标语：Play for yourself. / Never dim your light. / Follow your heart. / Be yourself and shine on. / Music knows no gender.

画面选择：打破被质疑、被偏见的视觉符号——女性在音乐舞台自信绽放（闪耀）。

反应描写：不要空洞的情绪堆砌

  • 体态：背挺直、眼睛闪闪发亮（hunched shoulders relaxed, spine straightened）
  • 内心：决心不让 Deepak 影响自己，要靠能力而非性别赢得机会
  • 回忆：与 tabla 的相遇之旅——指尖起泡、清晨练习、掌握第一个复杂节拍的喜悦

范文 · 标语 quote 版

One of them was illustrated with a quote, "Never dim your light." For that moment, Anjali was transported back to her first encounter with the tabla. She remembered how she was mesmerized by the beautiful sounds, created by dancing fingers. She knew that tabla was meant for her.

范文 · 图像版

With a closer look, her eyes were fixed on one particular poster featuring a girl highlighted in the spotlight. Though facing away from the audience, her silhouette still conveyed a strong sense of determination. Instantly, Anjali felt a surge of power rushing through her veins. "What if I were on the stage, playing tabla and immersed in the breathtaking rhythm of every strike?" she found herself wondering irresistibly.
【文本框】课堂批注
讲评①：标语扣牢「音乐无性别」，反应写具体——体态 / 内心 / 回忆。

Writing Clinic 2

讲评② 态度转变 · 认真训练

过渡总起句（时间流逝）

In the days that followed / In the following days / With each passing day / As time slipped away / As time ticked away unnoticed / Day in, day out / As days merged into weeks

训练动作表达

throw herself heart and soul into practice / immerse herself completely in the practice / bury herself in endless practice / pour all her passion into tireless practice / redouble her efforts into endless practice / go all out for the recital

关键提醒：除了坚持，还要「无视偏见」

对他人质疑置之不理、不分心——这是本段区别于泛泛而谈的关键：

… deaf to all outside noise. / ignoring all the prejudice and bias. / all the noise faded into the background. / completely absorbed in her own musical world.

As days slipped away, she buried herself in the realm of rhythm, leaving only sweat (and callouses) to bear witness to her devotion.
【文本框】课堂批注
讲评②：过渡句 + 训练表达 + 「无视偏见」结合文本。

Writing Clinic 3

讲评③ recital 到来 · 表演成功

转场句（训练 → 演出）

The day finally came. / Finally came the big day. / Finally came her long-awaited day. / Sweat and calluses bore witness to her dedication. / Before she even realized it, the long-awaited recital arrived, and it was her time to shine.

要点

  • 如果笔墨够，不写观众也可以
  • 但一定要写表演成功
  • 可写：登台前的深呼吸 / 指尖流淌的美妙乐音 / 观众惊叹
Finally came the big day. Standing on stage, she took a deep breath to get rid of lingering doubts and raised her hands in mid-air. The next second, everyone was captivated by the beautiful sounds flowing from her fingers. On the decisive stage, every single strike of hers blossomed into a breathless, flawless performance, leaving everyone present in awe.
【文本框】课堂批注
讲评③：转场自然，表演成功必写。

Writing Clinic 4

讲评④ Deepak 道歉 + Anjali 的反应

互动设计要点

  • 两人的互动是否需要详细描写？——可详可略，视笔墨而定
  • Anjali 的反应：惊讶或困惑？两人之间的坚冰融化
  • 老师挑选她参加音乐会、音乐会表演是否是重点？——不是，重点在和解与成长
  • 妙招：呼应第一段海报标语

范文

"Very impressive," he remarked awkwardly, admitting "I was just jealous because tabla should be a boy's thing." Anjali shrugged, "You know there is no such thing." … Under the spotlight, the set of tabla gleamed on the stage, mirroring her own radiant performance just minutes earlier. [呼应 poster]

点睛：Anjali's heart swelled. Looking back at Deepak and the rest of the boys, she read genuine admiration in their silent eyes. Her victory was not merely securing a spot in Mr. Zakir's concert—it was showing them all that music knew no gender, belonging only to those who gave it their sweat and soul.

【文本框】课堂批注
讲评④：道歉互动可详可略，主旨回扣海报「音乐无性别」。

Writing Clinic 5

讲评⑤ 主旨升华

主旨是什么？

  • 不是弥合两人的关系
  • 是相信自己，坚持梦想
  • 是女孩子可以做成功自己想做的任何事情
  • 必须回扣第一段的海报

范文（女孩可以做任何事）

Anjali's outstanding performance left no one surprised when they heard Mr. Zakir's pick — Anjali, the girl, the star student — for his next concert. Since then, whenever Mr. Zakir asked questions in class, Anjali was the first to respond confidently. The once self-doubting girl had vanished and was replaced by a brand-new Anjali, who firmly believed that girls could achieve anything they set their minds to.

范文（相信自己）：Among countless compliments, Anjali heard a little sweet voice, "Bravo, Anjali!" A little girl looked up at her, eyes wide with surprise: "I didn't know girls could play tabla." "Girls can do everything boys do," Anjali beamed, "Never dim your light."

【文本框】课堂批注
讲评⑤：主旨 = 相信自己 + 女孩能做任何事，回扣海报。

Writing Clinic 1

讲评① 海报内容 + Anjali 的反应

海报标语：必须扣牢文章

反思：Anjali 情绪低落的真正原因是因性别被质疑、被偏见而自我怀疑，而不是对自己没有信心。因此标语不能泛泛鼓励「加油 / 坚持」。

推荐标语：Play for yourself. / Never dim your light. / Follow your heart. / Be yourself and shine on. / Music knows no gender.

画面选择：打破被质疑、被偏见的视觉符号——女性在音乐舞台自信绽放（闪耀）。

反应描写：不要空洞的情绪堆砌

  • 体态：背挺直、眼睛闪闪发亮（hunched shoulders relaxed, spine straightened）
  • 内心：决心不让 Deepak 影响自己，要靠能力而非性别赢得机会
  • 回忆：与 tabla 的相遇之旅——指尖起泡、清晨练习、掌握第一个复杂节拍的喜悦

范文 · 标语 quote 版

One of them was illustrated with a quote, "Never dim your light." For that moment, Anjali was transported back to her first encounter with the tabla. She remembered how she was mesmerized by the beautiful sounds, created by dancing fingers. She knew that tabla was meant for her.

范文 · 图像版

With a closer look, her eyes were fixed on one particular poster featuring a girl highlighted in the spotlight. Though facing away from the audience, her silhouette still conveyed a strong sense of determination. Instantly, Anjali felt a surge of power rushing through her veins. "What if I were on the stage, playing tabla and immersed in the breathtaking rhythm of every strike?" she found herself wondering irresistibly.
【文本框】课堂批注
讲评①：标语扣牢「音乐无性别」，反应写具体——体态 / 内心 / 回忆。

Writing Clinic 2

讲评② 态度转变 · 认真训练

过渡总起句（时间流逝）

In the days that followed / In the following days / With each passing day / As time slipped away / As time ticked away unnoticed / Day in, day out / As days merged into weeks

训练动作表达

throw herself heart and soul into practice / immerse herself completely in the practice / bury herself in endless practice / pour all her passion into tireless practice / redouble her efforts into endless practice / go all out for the recital

关键提醒：除了坚持，还要「无视偏见」

对他人质疑置之不理、不分心——这是本段区别于泛泛而谈的关键：

… deaf to all outside noise. / ignoring all the prejudice and bias. / all the noise faded into the background. / completely absorbed in her own musical world.

As days slipped away, she buried herself in the realm of rhythm, leaving only sweat (and callouses) to bear witness to her devotion.
【文本框】课堂批注
讲评②：过渡句 + 训练表达 + 「无视偏见」结合文本。

Writing Clinic 3

讲评③ recital 到来 · 表演成功

转场句（训练 → 演出）

The day finally came. / Finally came the big day. / Finally came her long-awaited day. / Sweat and calluses bore witness to her dedication. / Before she even realized it, the long-awaited recital arrived, and it was her time to shine.

要点

  • 如果笔墨够，不写观众也可以
  • 但一定要写表演成功
  • 可写：登台前的深呼吸 / 指尖流淌的美妙乐音 / 观众惊叹
Finally came the big day. Standing on stage, she took a deep breath to get rid of lingering doubts and raised her hands in mid-air. The next second, everyone was captivated by the beautiful sounds flowing from her fingers. On the decisive stage, every single strike of hers blossomed into a breathless, flawless performance, leaving everyone present in awe.
【文本框】课堂批注
讲评③：转场自然，表演成功必写。

Writing Clinic 4

讲评④ Deepak 道歉 + Anjali 的反应

互动设计要点

  • 两人的互动是否需要详细描写？——可详可略，视笔墨而定
  • Anjali 的反应：惊讶或困惑？两人之间的坚冰融化
  • 老师挑选她参加音乐会、音乐会表演是否是重点？——不是，重点在和解与成长
  • 妙招：呼应第一段海报标语

范文

"Very impressive," he remarked awkwardly, admitting "I was just jealous because tabla should be a boy's thing." Anjali shrugged, "You know there is no such thing." … Under the spotlight, the set of tabla gleamed on the stage, mirroring her own radiant performance just minutes earlier. [呼应 poster]

点睛：Anjali's heart swelled. Looking back at Deepak and the rest of the boys, she read genuine admiration in their silent eyes. Her victory was not merely securing a spot in Mr. Zakir's concert—it was showing them all that music knew no gender, belonging only to those who gave it their sweat and soul.

【文本框】课堂批注
讲评④：道歉互动可详可略，主旨回扣海报「音乐无性别」。

Writing Clinic 5

讲评⑤ 主旨升华

主旨是什么？

  • 不是弥合两人的关系
  • 是相信自己，坚持梦想
  • 是女孩子可以做成功自己想做的任何事情
  • 必须回扣第一段的海报

范文（女孩可以做任何事）

Anjali's outstanding performance left no one surprised when they heard Mr. Zakir's pick — Anjali, the girl, the star student — for his next concert. Since then, whenever Mr. Zakir asked questions in class, Anjali was the first to respond confidently. The once self-doubting girl had vanished and was replaced by a brand-new Anjali, who firmly believed that girls could achieve anything they set their minds to.

范文（相信自己）：Among countless compliments, Anjali heard a little sweet voice, "Bravo, Anjali!" A little girl looked up at her, eyes wide with surprise: "I didn't know girls could play tabla." "Girls can do everything boys do," Anjali beamed, "Never dim your light."

【文本框】课堂批注
讲评⑤：主旨 = 相信自己 + 女孩能做任何事，回扣海报。

Para.1 · 海报与蜕变

Stepping out of the bathroom, Anjali saw some inspiring posters on the wall. One depicted a young woman holding a tabla with the words, "Rhythm is not gendered — perseverance crafts the artist." The message resonated deeply with her, washing away her doubts and fears. Anjali's spirit rekindled; she straightened her back, her resolve hardening. She decided then and there that her performance at the recital would speak for her dedication and talent. With each beat she practiced, her confidence grew. By the day of the recital, Anjali was more prepared than ever, turning her anxiety into an electrifying performance that captivated everyone in the audience.

Para.2 · 道歉与升华

After the recital, Deepak walked up to Anjali. His expression was one of awe and remorse. "Anjali, I was wrong," he said earnestly. "Your playing was the highlight of the evening. I'm sorry for how I treated you; you truly are a remarkable musician." Anjali nodded, accepting his apology with a gracious smile. "Thank you, Deepak. Let's both keep pushing ourselves and support each other as musicians, regardless of anything else," she responded. This moment was more than a personal victory; it was a testament to her belief that with hard work and belief in oneself, any barrier could be overcome.

Student Work

学生佳作 · 对比讲评

佳作 1

Para.1：Among them, one dazzling tabla poster stood out with a bold slogan reading "Play for yourself." At these uplifting words, Anjali felt a blazing flame of determination ignited in her chest: She will secure the concert opportunity, not by her gender but by her competence. Ultimately, the decisive recital came. Anjali, who left the taunts and teases aside, braved the stage and played the tabla freely and fluidly. Mr. Zakir announced the winner — Anjali! She made it!

Para.2：After the recital, Deepak walked up to Anjali. Instead of wearing the usual mean expression, he drooped his head in shame, murmuring, "Your performance deserves the honor." Hearing the heartfelt praise, Anjali couldn't help but curve her lips. Heart brimming with a new dose of confidence, Anjali dedicated herself to the demanding practice sessions with Mr. Zakir. She now was fully convinced that music offers equality for all, regardless of gender.

佳作 2

Para.1：One striking image caught her eye: a young woman, immersed in playing the tabla, beneath the bold slogan "Gender is no barrier, simply go and play!" These powerful words reignited a blazing flame of determination within her. Rather than surrender to doubt, she resolved to redouble her efforts and fight back, proving that her skill, not her gender, should define her success. In the days that followed, Anjali dedicated herself completely, sharpening her craft and inching ever closer to perfection.

Para.2：After the recital, Deepak walked up to Anjali. His sudden presence took Anjali off guard, prompting her to assume a defensive posture. But this time, she saw a change in him. Gone was his usual mean sneer; his head was bowed in shame. "Your performance was truly remarkable—I regret my earlier words," he stammered, visibly embarrassed. As Anjali accepted his apology with a forgiving smile, a warm current surged through her heart. Indeed, she had proven that a girl could excel in anything she set her heart on.

佳作 3

Para.1："Music knows no gender." "Savor the charm of music!" She roamed her gaze on them. The energetic words somehow put a bandage on her broken heart, mutely telling her, "you should give it another try." And so she did. Anjali channelled double efforts into practice and her efforts paid off.

点评：标语直接点题「音乐无性别」；「put a bandage on her broken heart」比喻新颖，情感过渡自然。篇幅略短，若补足表演细节与 Deepak 互动更完整。

学生佳作：三篇对比，观察标语扣题、体态与内心描写、主旨回扣的差异。
